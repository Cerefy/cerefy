# Cerefy Strategic Research Sources

Research captured 2026-08-14 for the strategic audit. These are external-source findings, not claims about Cerefy.

## Competitive landscape

1. **Aily Labs — Decision Intelligence.** Aily positions itself as a real-time decision-intelligence platform that connects siloed enterprise data to produce actionable insights and recommendations. It markets functional modules including finance, supply chain, quality, procurement, regulatory affairs, ethics/business integrity, and people. Its site identifies enterprise-grade security/compliance signals including SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO certifications. Source: https://www.ailylabs.com/

2. **Arabic.AI / Tarjama — Arabic-first enterprise AI.** Arabic.AI markets an Arabic-first enterprise suite spanning assistants, translation, OCR, and speech. It claims deployment choices including cloud, private VPC, on-premises, and air-gapped modes; native Arabic/dialect and code-switching capability; RAG-grounded assistants; and enterprise controls such as SSO/SAML, RBAC, audit logs, data residency, and PII controls. Source: https://www.arabic.ai/

3. **Arabic.AI launch coverage.** Wamda reported in April 2025 that Arabic.AI launched a B2B agentic platform based on the Pronoia model, with workflow integration, custom solutions, cloud/on-prem/API deployment, and use across financial services, healthcare, legal, and media. Treat company performance assertions in this article as company claims, not independently verified benchmarks. Source: https://www.wamda.com/en/2025/04/arabicai-launches-mena-b2b-agentic-ai-platform

## Regulatory and market context

4. **Saudi Arabia.** Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA) describes its national role across data/AI and its National Strategy for Data & AI. Its official website records ongoing data/AI regulatory and capability-building initiatives. Source: https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/default.aspx

5. **United Arab Emirates.** The UAE’s official 2024 international AI policy identifies transparency/checkpoints, accountability, security and safety, personal-data/privacy protection, explainability, resilience, and human values as key priorities. It applies to public and private sector companies, AI developers, consumers, and international partners. Source: https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/policy/details/uae-s-international-stance-on-artificial-intelligence-policy

## Audit implications

- Cerefy cannot credibly compete as a general Arabic AI suite or generic decision-intelligence platform on breadth.
- Its defensible path is a narrower evidence-to-decision-to-approved-workflow product for Arabic/MENA enterprise operations, with source traceability, human approval, outcome recording, and governance as defaults.
- Sovereign/VPC/on-premise deployment, formal model governance, data-residency options, and evaluation evidence are enterprise-market requirements to plan for, not capabilities currently present in Cerefy.
